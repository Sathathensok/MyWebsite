Who I Am

I'm Sathathen Sok — a Cambodian logistics professional, systems specialist, and automation developer with over seven years of experience working across Southeast Asia's supply chain industry.

I didn't plan a career in logistics. But after years of working through complex operational challenges at DSV, DB Schenker, and Kuehne + Nagel, I've developed a genuine interest in one specific problem: how do you make large, complicated systems work reliably — and how do you improve them without disrupting the people who depend on them?

That question is what drives most of what I do. I work at the intersection of operations, technology, and people — trying to bring a bit more clarity and structure to environments that often have too little of both.

Working Life

My career began at DSV in air freight operations, where I developed a strong foundation in time-sensitive logistics and learned early that in this industry, the gap between a good process and a bad one is measured in client trust, not just efficiency metrics.

At DB Schenker, I took on responsibility for the company's largest sea export account — a role that required balancing complex client expectations with the practical realities of day-to-day operations. It sharpened my ability to manage stakeholders at multiple levels while keeping the operational side running smoothly.

Since April 2022, I've been with Kuehne + Nagel as Thailand Cluster Sea Logistics and Process Order Management lead, covering Cambodia, Thailand, and Myanmar. My work spans process improvement, automation development, system rollouts, and cross-country team alignment. The scope is broad by design — regional operations rarely fit neatly into a single job description.

Some of the work I'm most proud of: helping position Cambodia's sea logistics operation within the global top 10 KPI rankings, deploying automation initiatives that generated a 12+ FTE productivity equivalent and $10K + in monthly savings, and achieving 86% auto-invoicing adoption within the Cambodian target market. None of those outcomes came from technology alone — they came from the combination of the right tools, clear processes, and teams that understood why the change mattered.

Study Life

I hold a Bachelor's degree in International Economics from the Royal University of Phnom Penh, where I served as Class President, and a second Bachelor's in Enterprise Management from the Royal University of Law and Economics. Both programmes gave me a grounding in economics and business fundamentals that I've drawn on consistently throughout my career.

I later pursued postgraduate study in China, first at The University of Nottingham Ningbo China and then at the University of International Business and Economics in Beijing, where I completed my MBA with a CGPA of 3.9/4.0. Studying in China added a dimension to my understanding of international trade and cross-cultural business that classroom theory alone couldn't provide — and living and working across multiple countries has reinforced that practical experience consistently outweighs credentials, even if credentials help you get in the room.

Beyond formal study, I hold certifications in UiPath RPA, SOAP and REST API development, VBA programming, and Six Sigma (Lean Black Belt), and I continue to invest in technical skills alongside my operational work.

What I'm Building

Alongside my full-time role, I'm developing a portfolio of independent work focused on three areas where I believe I can add real value for businesses at the SME and growth stage — particularly in Cambodia and the wider Southeast Asian market.

  • Automation Development — Designing and building RPA solutions, VBA tools, and workflow automations using UiPath, Power Automate, and related platforms. The goal is always the same: reduce the time your team spends on repetitive, rule-based tasks so they can focus on work that actually requires human judgment.
  • Business Consulting — Working with founders and operations leads to map their processes, identify bottlenecks, and build systems that scale. I focus on practical, implementable recommendations rather than frameworks that look good in a slide deck but stall in execution.
  • Web Development — Building clean, functional websites and web platforms that help businesses establish a credible digital presence and operate more efficiently online. Designed for real-world use, not just aesthetics.

If any of these areas are relevant to a challenge you're working through, I'm always open to a direct conversation about whether I can help.
